macOS App for Receipts & Warranty Records

Smart receipt extraction & purchase records in one place.

Cute Cat Pomodoro turns physical receipts into organized digital records. Scan with camera or pick from Photos, extract line items automatically with Vision OCR, group items by merchant, and keep track of return deadlines and warranties.

Key Features

Built for fast scanning and complete privacy

On-Device Vision OCR

Scan receipts using your camera or upload images. Cute Cat Pomodoro automatically parses merchant names, dates, line items, and totals using Apple's Vision framework.

Grouped Receipt Cards

Items are organized into receipt cards (Costco, Target, Apple, etc.) so you can view all items inside a single receipt, or merge and split receipts easily.

Pinch-to-Zoom Viewer

Pinch out on any receipt image to inspect fine details, store return policies, or original total breakdowns.
