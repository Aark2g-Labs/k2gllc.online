Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kindoop

How does giving an item work?

Tap the center Give button, take or upload up to 6 photos of your item, review the automatically suggested category and age details, and tap Give Away.

Is Kindoop completely free?

Yes. Every item on Kindoop is passed along for free between parents in your local community. There are no listing fees, buying fees, or transactional payments.

Is my exact address shown to other parents?

No. Kindoop only shows an approximate neighborhood and distance (e.g. "0.5 mi away"). Your exact address remains private until you choose a recipient and coordinate handoff details via in-app messaging.

How do handoffs work?

When a parent requests your item, you can review their request under the Requests tab, choose to accept, and open a private messaging channel to arrange a comfortable public or porch pickup.

How do I report an inappropriate listing?

On any item detail screen, tap the ... menu in the upper right corner and select Report this listing. Our team reviews all reported listings quietly.

How do I delete my listing or profile data?

Go to Profile → My listings to delete any active items. To delete your profile or account, visit Settings or email our support team.
