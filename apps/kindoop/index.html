Hyperlocal Neighborhood App for Kids' Things

Keep kindness in the loop.

Kindoop is a free, local place for parents to pass along children's toys, clothes, books, and baby gear. Give items freely to nearby families and discover good things for your growing children.

Core Design Principles

Built for neighborhood trust and frictionless giving

Fast, Assisted Giving

Pass things along effortlessly. Start with a photo, get helpful details automatically, and list your item in seconds.

Hyperlocal & Privacy-First

Discover items nearby filtered by distance and neighborhood. Your exact home address is never shown publicly.

Kindness over Commerce

Every item on Kindoop is completely FREE. No pricing, no negotiations, and no transactional clutter—just parents helping parents.
