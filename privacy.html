PaperTrail Privacy Policy

Your privacy and receipt data are fully protected.

PaperTrail is designed around local device processing and complete data ownership. Learn how we handle permissions and data.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

1. On-Device Storage & Data Ownership

PaperTrail stores your receipt images, line items, warranties, and product documents locally on your iOS device using SwiftData and encrypted local file storage. Your personal data belongs to you and is never sold, rented, or tracked by third parties.

2. Camera & Photo Library Access

PaperTrail requests access to your iOS camera and photo library solely to allow you to scan physical receipts, attach product photos, or select existing receipt images from your library. Photo data remains on your device.

3. On-Device Vision OCR Processing

Text recognition and receipt parsing are performed directly on your iOS device using Apple's Vision framework. Receipt scanning does not require an internet connection.

4. Optional AI Enhancements

If AI Receipt Enhancement is enabled by the user, PaperTrail may send OCR text snippets to OpenRouter AI services to improve merchant, item name, and price recognition before saving. This service operates strictly for extraction accuracy and does not profile users.

5. Data Deletion & Export Rights

You retain full control over your data. You can export your data at any time via Settings → Prepare Export, or delete all records and account information via Settings → Delete All Data or Delete Account.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact K2G LLC at contact@k2gllc.online.
